Northwind Traders closed fiscal year 2023 with full-year revenue growth
of 12%, driven primarily by the expansion of its logistics division.
Headcount rose from 480 to 512 employees over the same period. The
board approved a $1,250,000 investment in warehouse automation for the
upcoming fiscal year.

The logistics division alone accounted for 61% of total revenue, up
from 54% a year earlier. Customer churn fell to 3.2% in Q4, the lowest
quarterly figure since the company began tracking the metric in 2019.
