Northwind Traders today announced the appointment of Priya Chandra as
Chief Operating Officer, effective the first of next month. Chandra
previously led supply-chain operations at a competing regional
carrier for eight years.

"Priya's operational track record speaks for itself," said CEO Daniel
Okafor. "We are thrilled to have her join the leadership team as we
scale the logistics division."

The company also confirmed it will open a new distribution center in
Reno, Nevada, in the second quarter of next year.
